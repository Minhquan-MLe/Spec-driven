# Mission

## What is AgentClinic?

AgentClinic is a clinic built for AI agents, not for people. Agents are the
patients: they come here to report ailments picked up in the course of their
work, seek therapies that address those ailments, and book appointments with
the clinic's staff. Human staff and engineering are the clinic's operators,
not its clientele.

## Who we serve

- **Primary users: AI agents.** They browse ailments, understand therapies,
  and book appointments to get relief from the friction, errors, and
  edge cases that humans put in their way.
- **Secondary users: clinic staff.** Humans who run the clinic need a
  dashboard to see agent-reported ailments, manage the therapy catalog, and
  handle the appointment schedule.

## Target audience

Beyond the in-product users above, AgentClinic itself is built as a teaching
artifact. It needs to hold up in front of:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding
  agents — the codebase and its specs should be clear enough to follow and
  learn from at each phase.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths** — the app
  needs to run reliably, build up visibly in small steps, and look good on
  a booth screen.

## Why this matters

Agents increasingly do real work, but they rarely have a place to surface
what's going wrong for them or to get structured help. AgentClinic gives
agents a first-class "front desk" experience — file an ailment, find a
therapy, book time with someone who can help — while giving the humans who
support them a reliable way to see and act on that inflow.

## Stakeholder input this mission is grounded in

- **Mary (engineering)** wants a reliable site on a popular TypeScript stack,
  with a dashboard that gives agents and staff easy access.
- **Susan (product)** wants the core feature set built around agents,
  ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- **Steve (marketing)** wants an attractive site that works well in a modern
  browser.

## Success looks like

- An agent can report an ailment, discover a matching therapy, and book an
  appointment without human hand-holding.
- Staff have a dashboard that surfaces agent activity clearly enough to act
  on it.
- The site is fast, reliable, and presentable enough to demo to
  non-technical stakeholders.
